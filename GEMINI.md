# Writing & Copy Guidelines for Jargonet

Always follow the Human Editor principles when writing or editing any copy, documentation, commit messages, or UI text in Jargonet:

1. **No AI Patterns or Slop:**
   - Ban words like: delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving.
   - Cut throat-clearing openers, binary contrasts ("Not X, but Y"), colon reveals, and fake-profound kickers.
   - Avoid em dashes as a default rhythm crutch.

2. **Active, Concrete, and Grounded:**
   - Lead with facts, mechanisms, and concrete actions.
   - Preserve real edge, bluntness, and honesty. Jargonet is a working, private peer-to-peer system, not corporate marketing puffery.
